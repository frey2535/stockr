Stockr is field inventory for contractor warehouses and service fleets.

Scan barcodes or photograph a product on the job, move material between the shop and trucks, receive purchase orders, and keep an activity log the office can export.

Each company gets its own workspace and plan. Starter is free for a small shop. Pro and Fleet add locations, catalog items, and seats.

In this Google Play app, Pro and Fleet are purchased with Google Play Billing. The website uses Stripe for the same plans.

Package name: org.currentflowconsulting.stockr

Camera is used to read barcodes and, if you choose Photo ID, to identify a product. Photos used for identification are sent to Stockr’s servers and vision providers as described in the privacy policy.

Account deletion: Settings → Your data → Delete workspace, or email stockr@currentflowconsulting.org.
