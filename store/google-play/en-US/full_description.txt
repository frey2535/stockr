Stockr is field inventory for contractor warehouses and service fleets.

Scan barcodes on the job, move material between the shop and trucks, receive purchase orders, and keep an activity log the office can export.

Each company gets its own workspace and plan. Starter is free for a small shop. Pro and Fleet add locations, catalog items, and seats. Subscribe on stockr.currentflowconsulting.org — billing is not an in-app Play purchase.

Package name: org.currentflowconsulting.stockr

You can also install the same app from the APK on the website if you do not use Google Play.
